Dear October,

You found me tired in ways I don't know how to explain.

The year is almost over, yet there are dreams I made in January that I can barely talk about without feeling like I've disappointed myself. I thought I would be further by now. I thought by this age, life would make a little more sense.

But here I am, still studying, still worrying about money, still pretending some days that I know exactly what I'm doing.

The strangest part of growing up is realizing that your parents were also figuring things out while raising you. They had fears they never told you about, bills they didn't know how to pay, and dreams they quietly buried so you could have a chance at yours.

And somehow, you are now old enough to understand their silence.

October, if you can give me anything, let it be the courage to keep going without hating myself for how long it's taking. Let me stop measuring my life against people whose struggles I know nothing about.

I don't need everything to work out tomorrow. I just want to reach a day when I can look back at these difficult years and whisper, *I'm glad I didn't give up on myself.*

And if that day takes longer than I hope, remind me that I am still allowed to enjoy the little things while I'm getting there.

Because I don't want to spend my whole life waiting for happiness to begin.

Sincerely,

Someone who is still becoming.
