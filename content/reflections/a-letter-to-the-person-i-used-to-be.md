Dear Younger Me,

I wish I could tell you how things turn out.

I wish I could tell you which dreams come true, which friendships survive, and whether all those nights you spent worrying eventually make sense.

But I am writing to you from a place where I still have questions of my own.

I thought growing up meant becoming fearless. Instead, it has taught me how to carry fear quietly. How to smile at people while trying to figure out my life. How to watch others move ahead and resist the temptation to believe that I have been left behind.

You were so eager to grow up. You thought adulthood meant freedom, money, and finally being able to give your family everything they deserved.

Nobody told us that sometimes the hardest part of loving our family is knowing how much they need, and how little we can offer while we are still finding our feet.

I am sorry for the times I have treated you like a disappointment. For every dream I called foolish because it took too long. For believing that our worth depended on how quickly we became successful.

You were never supposed to have everything figured out.

You were just a child with a big heart, hoping life would be kind.

I hope you forgive me for forgetting that.

And if I could ask one thing of you, it would be this: please don't be so eager to become someone that you forget to be yourself.

One day, we might have the money. We might get the degree, find the work, and build the life we keep imagining. I hope we do.

But even if the journey looks different from what we planned, I hope we never become people who measure everything in money, including our own happiness.

I hope we still laugh easily. I hope we still notice when someone is hurting. I hope we still call home just to hear a familiar voice.

And I hope that when we finally become the person we have spent years trying to be, we will be gentle enough to recognize the younger version of ourselves in someone who is still struggling.

Because I am beginning to understand something.

**Maybe growing up isn't about becoming the person you once dreamed of being. Maybe it is about learning to love the person you are while you are still on your way.**

With love,

The person you were trying so hard to become.
