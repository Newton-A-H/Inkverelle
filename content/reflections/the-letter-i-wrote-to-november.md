Dear November,

Please, be gentle with me.

I have spent most of this year trying to become someone I can be proud of. Some days, I think I am getting there. Other days, I sit quietly in my room and wonder how a person can try so hard and still feel so far from the life they imagined.

January feels like another lifetime ago. I remember the promises I made to myself, the plans I wrote down, and the quiet confidence that this would finally be my year. I thought by now, some things would have changed. I thought I would have more answers, fewer worries, and perhaps a little more money in my account.

But here I am, still figuring things out.

And if I am honest, November, I am tired.

Not the kind of tired that sleep can fix. I mean the tiredness of being strong for too long, of telling people you are fine because explaining the truth would take more energy than you have left. The tiredness of watching life move forward while some parts of you still feel stuck in places you desperately wanted to leave.

There are things I never told anyone this year. Disappointments I laughed about in public and cried over in private. Opportunities I prayed for that never came. People I thought would stay who slowly became strangers. Nights when I questioned my abilities, my decisions, and sometimes even the purpose of all this trying.

Yet somehow, I am still here.

Maybe that deserves more credit than I have given myself.

November, I don't need you to make me rich before the year ends. I don't need a miracle that suddenly fixes every broken thing. I only ask that you give me a little peace. Let me sleep without carrying tomorrow's worries into tonight. Let me find reasons to smile that have nothing to do with money, achievement, or proving that I am doing well.

And if there are still lessons I must learn, please don't let them cost me the last pieces of kindness I have left.

I want to become successful, yes. I want to make my family proud. I want to reach a point where the people who sacrificed for me can finally rest a little easier. I want to look back at these difficult years and know that they were taking me somewhere.

But I don't want to lose myself on the way there.

I don't want to become so desperate for a better life that I forget to live the one I have. I don't want to spend my youth being ashamed of my circumstances, or mistake a slow beginning for a failed story.

And please, November, remind me to call home.

Remind me that the people I love are growing older while I am busy chasing a future I hope will make everything worthwhile. Remind me that there are blessings I have overlooked simply because they arrived looking ordinary. A familiar voice. A shared meal. A friend who checks on me without needing anything. Another morning in which I am given the chance to try again.

I think I have spent too much time asking life when it will finally become easier.

Perhaps I should sometimes ask what it is still giving me, even now.

So, November, here is what I want you to know: I am entering your days with unfinished plans, unanswered prayers, and a heart that has been disappointed more than once. I am not the person I thought I would be by this point in the year.

But I am not giving up on that person, either.

I am learning that there is no shame in beginning again. No shame in taking longer. No shame in being twenty-something and still searching for your place in a world that seems to reward everyone else first.

If all I manage to do this month is keep going, love my people a little better, forgive myself a little more, and find the courage to believe in tomorrow, then perhaps that will be enough.

Perhaps I have been too harsh with the person who has carried me this far.

And when December comes asking what I achieved, I hope I can tell it that I survived some things nobody knew about. That I kept my heart open after disappointment. That I continued to dream, even when reality gave me reasons not to.

Most importantly, I hope I can say that somewhere between everything I wanted and everything I endured, I learned to be kinder to myself.

So, dear November, come as you are.

Just don't let me leave you believing that my life was on hold because it wasn't perfect.

I have spent enough days waiting to become someone worthy of happiness.

**This month, I want to remember that I am a human being, not a project that must be completed before the year ends.**

And if nothing else changes, let that be the thing that finally does.

Yours, still hoping,

Someone who is learning to begin again.
