Dear August,

You arrived with your full hands and no apology. By the time I noticed, you had already rearranged the afternoon light and left peaches ripening on the counter.

I wanted to be the kind of person who made a plan for every bright day. Instead I learned how much can happen when the plan is simply to stay.
