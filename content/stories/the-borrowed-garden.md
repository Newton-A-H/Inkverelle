The garden belonged to the empty house on the corner, though nobody had seen its owner in years. Inez watered it anyway.

She began with the rosemary because it looked thirsty. Then the roses, then the beds of herbs that had grown wild along the fence. The gate was always open in the morning. She thought of this as an invitation.

By spring the garden was loud with color, and someone had left a small clay pot on the step. Inside it, a note said: Thank you for keeping the place alive.
