Beneath the weight of empty skies,
A shadow stirs, but never cries.
His silence echoes louder still,
A broken heart that can't be healed.

He walks through days like faded light,
Each step a ghost, a silent fight.
His thoughts are tangled, dark and thin,
Searching for a place to begin.

Hope once whispered soft and clear,
Now lies like dust, too far, too near.
In crowded rooms, he stands alone,
A silhouette, a king unthrown.

And though his heart still yearns to fly,
His wings are torn, and so he sighs.
Yet in the dark, a spark may bloom—
A fractured man, but not yet doomed.
