The rhythm of a dying heart
Is slow, like fading light,
Each beat a tremor in the dark,
Each pulse a whispered fight.

Once it danced with joy and fire,
Now it stutters, weak and cold,
A flame reduced to fragile embers,
A story left untold.

The hands that held it close and warm
Have let it fall apart,
And now it beats against the storm,
A drum that’s lost its art.

It remembers once, the laughing days,
The warmth of words, the sound of praise,
Now silence is its bitter friend,
And echoes taunt the edges’ bend.

Every thump, a hollow prayer,
A plea to feel alive again,
But all it finds is empty air,
And memories that wane.

Yet in its hollow, rhythmic cry,
A truth begins to stir—
The heart will break, but it won’t die,
It simply learns to blur.

For even in the aching still,
The sorrow’s depth, the love that’s lost,
The dying rhythm carries will—
A ghost that bears the cost.

And when the final beat is done,
The song will have its end,
But in the silence, something new—
A heart will rise again.
