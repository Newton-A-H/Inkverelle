A broken man I am, you see,
Shadows of what I used to be.
Pieces scattered, heart undone,
Seeking hope, yet finding none.

The weight of dreams that slipped away,
Haunt the night and blur the day.
Tears unspoken, scars unseen,
Life unraveling at the seam.

But in this ruin, I'll arise,
A phoenix born from shattered skies.
For every crack, a light can shine,
A broken man, but still divine.
