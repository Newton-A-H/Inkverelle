Shaliey, my dear, don't fear the light,
Though shadows creep in the quiet of night.
The world may seem vast, and hard to bear,
But know, sweet friend, I'm always there.

You are my Lilith, fierce and strong,
Though doubts may tell you you don’t belong.
In every heartbeat, there's a flame,
A spark of life that bears your name.

The wind may howl, the storm may rage,
But don’t be trapped in fear’s dark cage.
For in your heart, there's strength untold,
A soul so pure, a spirit bold.

Step forward, friend, and take your stand,
You'll always have a helping hand.
Though life feels scary, cruel, and wide,
I’ll walk with you, right by your side.

So when you're lost, and hope feels thin,
Remember, Lilith, from within,
There lives a warrior, kind and bright—
You are meant to live, to shine, to fight.
