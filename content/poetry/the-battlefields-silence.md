The echoes of the guns have died,
The smoke has cleared, and side by side,
They lie—those brave and fallen men,
Gone from the world, but not again.

The ground is scarred, the skies are gray,
Yet hope still whispers through the fray.
For in the quiet, seeds are sown,
And from the blood, new life has grown.

The tears we weep, they fall like rain,
But heal the earth, and ease the pain.
Though war has torn our souls apart,
A dawn of peace still fills the heart.

Though sorrow deepens every scar,
A light remains, not lost, but far.
So in the shadow of despair,
We find the strength to rise and care.
