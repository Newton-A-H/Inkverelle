The good ones smile, but never win,
Their hearts too soft, their hopes too thin.
The world is loud, it pulls and strains,
And kindness bleeds through endless pains.

They give, they love, they stand so tall,
But life keeps pushing them to fall.
In every fight, they lose the race,
While others thrive in empty grace.

Yet still they try, though shadows loom,
Believing light will pierce the gloom—
But in this world where wrong is spun,
The good are lost before they’ve won.
