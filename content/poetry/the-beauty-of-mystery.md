He drifts through shadows, quiet and calm,
A whispered verse, a wordless psalm.
In crowded rooms, he stands apart,
Guarding the riddles of his heart.

Eyes like embers, soft and deep,
Hold secrets he intends to keep.
Not cold nor distant, just unseen,
A hidden line, a shade between.

He speaks in hints, in quiet tones,
A puzzle pieced by thoughts alone.
For him, there’s beauty in the blur,
Where guess and wonder gently stir.

He savors questions left unsaid,
Preferring silence there instead.
To know him is a patient art,
Unravelling slowly, part by part.

So let them guess, let silence fall,
The mystery is worth it all.
